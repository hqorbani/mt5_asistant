# Usage Guide

To use the MetaTrader 5 Assistant, you can enter the following information:

- **Symbol**: The trading symbol.
- **Order Type**: The type of order (buy or sell).
- **Lot**: The lot size.
- **Entry Price**: The price at which the order is placed.
- **Stop Loss Price**: The stop loss price.
- **Take Profit Price**: The take profit price.

After entering the information, click on the "Calculate" button to see the results for:

- **Profit**: The profit of the order.
- **Loss**: The loss of the order.
- **Margin**: The margin required for the order.
- **Risk/Reward**: The risk/reward ratio of the order.
